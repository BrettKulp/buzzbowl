# Buzz Bowl Roadmap

Features and improvements planned for Buzz Bowl. Have an idea? [Open an issue](https://github.com/BrettKulp/buzzbowl/issues) to suggest adding it to the roadmap.

## Planned Features

### Full Game States
- Quarters with scoring
- Option for quarters based on number of plays or a timer

### Player Stats
- End-of-game offensive and defensive stats per player
- Rushing yards, tackles, passing yards, etc.

### Player Bases
- Assign each player a base that affects movement
- Attributes like speed, pushing strength, turning tendency, etc.

### Challenge Mode
- Mission-based challenges similar to Madden or Mario Kart DS Mission Mode
- Examples: score an 80-yard rushing touchdown, stop the offense from scoring, etc.

### Stadium and Player Customization
- Custom colors and designs for players and stadiums

### Tutorial
- A short into tutorial that tells the user how to play the game that can be revisited

### Sound Effects & Music
- Sound effects for tackles, touchdowns, whistles, etc.
- Background music

## Longer Term 
- Improved graphics with better assets possibly pixel art
- Multiplayer (single player experience being prioritized first)
